Section 2

Comparable Civic Tech Monetization

What others charge. What GCC can learn from them.

NYC 311 Analytics

Reference

NYC spends approximately $75M/year on 311 operations. Third-party analytics companies like Zencity charge $50K–$300K/year for civic analytics dashboards layered on top of 311 data.

Takeaway: There is a proven market for cities paying for analytics on community-reported data. GCC's data is higher quality than 311 reports.

SeeClickFix

Closest Comparable

Raised $2.3M, acquired by CivicPlus. Revenue model: SaaS platform for cities at $50K–$200K/year per municipality. Over 400 cities as clients.

Takeaway: SeeClickFix is essentially a fancier 311 without verification, AI, or gamification. GCC has all three. If SeeClickFix got 400 cities at $50K+, GCC has room.

Clean Streets LA

Reference

$3.5M annual budget for LASAN (LA Sanitation). Uses a mobile app for reporting but lacks the verification layer GCC has. No before/after documentation. No AI classification.

Takeaway: LA spends $3.5M on something GCC does better. This is the competitive benchmark to reference when talking about value.

The Honest Competitive Assessment

No one does what GCC does: verified before/after data with AI classification. This is genuinely unique. SeeClickFix is the closest comparison, and they are essentially a fancier 311 without verification, AI, or gamification. GCC's moat is real, but only if it is communicated clearly. Do not undersell the verification layer — it is the entire differentiator.

Section 3

Tax Write-Off Mechanics

IRS Publication 526 rules for 501(c)(3) donations. Know this cold.

IRS Publication 526 Rules

Donation Type Deduction Rule Limit
Cash donations 100% deductible Up to 60% of donor's AGI
In-kind (tickets, passes) Deductible at Fair Market Value (FMV) FMV = face value printed on ticket
Unsold sports tickets FMV is the face value Even if they would have gone unsold
Technology equipment FMV based on comparable market price Must document comparable pricing
Corporate donations 100% deductible Up to 25% of taxable income

What This Actually Means for Donors

1

A team donating 100 tickets at $50 face value = $5,000 deduction.

2

It costs them nothing (tickets were going unsold) but saves them $1,000–$1,750 in taxes (at 20–35% corporate rate).

3

This is the core pitch: "Your unsold inventory becomes a tax advantage AND community PR."

Receipting Requirement — Do Not Forget This

GCC must provide a written acknowledgment for any single donation over $250. The acknowledgment must include a statement that no goods or services were provided in return. Without this receipt, the donor cannot claim the deduction. Have a template ready before your first donation conversation.

Section 4

Meta / NVIDIA Pitch Strategy

What to say, what to ask for, and what to do if they say no.

Meta — Ray-Ban Smart Glasses

Why They Would Say Yes

Meta is desperately trying to make smart glasses mainstream. Environmental/community use case is better PR than "record your friends at brunch." GCC provides a real, documented, photographable use case. Meta needs to be seen as helpful, not creepy.

The Pitch (Use This Language)

"Your glasses help workers clean neighborhoods with both hands free. We provide before/after GPS-verified documentation of every cleanup. This is the use case that makes smart glasses look heroic."

What to Ask For

10–20 pairs of Ray-Ban Meta glasses for field workers. That is it. Small ask, big PR for them.

If They Say No

Approach Snapchat (Spectacles), or position any competitor glasses as "the smart glasses that actually care about community." This creates FOMO for Meta. Use the rejection as leverage.

Contact Path

Meta for Good program → meta.com/social-impact, or reach out via the Chicago Meta office directly.

NVIDIA — AI Hardware

Why They Would Say Yes

NVIDIA's "AI for Good" program needs real-world showcases. Environmental monitoring with on-device AI is exactly their narrative. GCC is a ready-made case study.

The Pitch (Use This Language)

"GCC runs 12-category waste classification on TFLite/CoreML today. With NVIDIA edge hardware (Jetson), we could do real-time classification during the documentation walk — no cloud roundtrip."

What to Ask For

2–3 Jetson Orin Nano units for edge AI processing. DGX access for model training. These are small costs to NVIDIA.

If They Say No

Honest assessment: the current on-device AI works fine without NVIDIA. This is a nice-to-have, not a blocker. Do not let a rejection slow you down.

Contact Path

NVIDIA Foundation → nvidia.com/en-us/foundation/, or the NVIDIA AI for Good program.

Section 8

What to Tell UChicago

Framing, opportunities, and talking points for the meeting.

The Framing (Memorize This)

GCC is a research-grade civic technology platform generating structured environmental data at the community level. We are not just a cleanup app. We are building Chicago's first comprehensive ground-truth dataset on neighborhood cleanliness. 2,000+ verified data points, 25-field schema, AI-classified, GPS-verified, with before/after documentation.

Research Collaboration Opportunities

Urban Studies

Cleanliness as a proxy for neighborhood investment. Gentrification pattern detection. Disinvestment mapping.

Computer Science

On-device waste classification models. LiDAR volume estimation accuracy. Edge AI deployment.

Public Policy

Impact of gamification on civic participation. Bounty economics and community engagement incentives.

Data Science

Predictive models for waste accumulation. Optimal cleanup routing algorithms. Spatial clustering.

Environmental Science

Waste composition analysis across community areas. Seasonal patterns. Illegal dumping detection.

Behavioral Economics

How point systems and badges affect sustained community engagement. Intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation.

What We Need from UChicago

  • ‣ Student researchers (data analysis, model improvement)
  • ‣ Credibility signal (UChicago partnership logo on materials)
  • ‣ Access to urban planning faculty connections
  • ‣ Potential IRB for human subjects research (gamification effectiveness study)

What UChicago Gets

  • ‣ Novel dataset for publications (no one else has this data)
  • ‣ Real-world AI/ML deployment case study
  • ‣ Community engagement research platform
  • ‣ Chicago-focused civic tech partnership for their own grant proposals

Talking Points for the Meeting

In order of importance. Hit all five.

1

Lead with data quality and quantity

2,000+ verified points, 25-field schema, AI-classified, GPS-verified. This is not a hobby project — it is a real dataset.

2

Emphasize the verification layer

No other platform has community-verified ground truth with before/after documentation. This is what makes the data publishable.

3

Position as research infrastructure, not an app

Do not demo the app. Talk about the data pipeline, the schema, the API. Academics care about methodology, not UI.

4

Ask about their existing research

Frame GCC as complementary to what they already do. "How could our data enhance your current urban planning research?"

5

Make a specific, low-commitment ask

"Give us 5 graduate students for one semester. We provide the data and API access. They publish what they find." This is easy for them to say yes to.
