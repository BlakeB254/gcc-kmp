  • Tier 1 (This Week) — Quick wins that require minimal effort and build your portfolio of partnerships. Start here. Navy Pier deadline March 30!

Priority labels in each table indicate both likelihood of success and strategic value:

  • MEDIUM — Good fit but requires more effort or longer timeline
  • LOW — Worth pursuing but lower urgency or uncertain outcome

Template Customization Tips

  • 1. Always personalize the first line. Reference something specific about the org (recent event, program, news article).
  • 2. Include a number. "2,000+ verified data points" or "77 community areas" lands harder than vague claims.
  • 3. Attach one before/after photo pair. Visual proof is worth more than any paragraph of text.
  • 4. Follow up within 7 days. Most nonprofit requests get lost. A polite follow-up doubles response rates.
  • 5. Reference existing partners. Once you land Tier 1 wins, mention them in Tier 2 outreach to build social proof.
