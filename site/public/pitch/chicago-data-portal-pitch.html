Section 1

Executive Summary

Global Community Cleaners (GCC) is a community-powered platform where residents, workers, and volunteers report, verify, and clean up environmental issues across Chicago's 77 community areas.

Unlike reactive 311 complaints, GCC generates proactive, verified, photo-documented data with AI-powered analysis, LiDAR depth scanning, and a 24-hour verification cycle. The platform transforms community engagement into structured, machine-readable environmental data that surpasses the granularity and reliability of traditional complaint-based systems.

The GCC mobile application enables residents to submit geotagged, photo-documented reports of environmental conditions using their smartphones. Each report undergoes on-device AI classification to categorize waste types, estimate severity, and—on devices equipped with LiDAR sensors—measure volume and depth of debris. Reports enter a 24-hour verification cycle where other community members re-visit the site to confirm, update, or mark conditions as resolved.

The platform currently covers all 77 community areas with over 2,000 data points, density-weighted to reflect actual environmental burden. This data is available through a SODA-compatible REST API, ready for integration with the Chicago Data Portal with zero additional engineering from the City.

GCC is building a sustainable revenue model through data licensing, consulting, and platform licensing — not seeking perpetual grants. Our community-driven approach generates data at zero cost to the city while creating a scalable business that can serve municipalities nationwide.

API Endpoints

GET /api/public/data-export

Primary data endpoint. Returns paginated community report records.

Query Parameters:
community_area Filter by area (1–77)
ward Filter by ward (1–50)
status Filter by status
$limit Results per page (max 1000)
$offset Pagination offset
$order Sort field and direction

Aggregation endpoint. Returns summary statistics by community area, ward, or time period.

Section 6

Value Proposition

What the City of Chicago gains from integrating GCC data into the Data Portal.

1

Richer Data

12 waste categories versus a single "Sanitation Code Complaint." Severity scores from 1 to 10. Weight and volume estimates for every report. GCC data is an order of magnitude more granular than 311 for environmental conditions.

2

Visual Evidence

Every GCC report includes geotagged photos. Volunteer cleanups include before/after photo pairs that document impact. 311 complaints include no photographic evidence. GCC provides proof, not just complaints.

3

Verification Layer

The 24-hour re-verification cycle means stale reports are caught and updated. A report with zero missed verifications is confirmed current. A report with several missed cycles is flagged as potentially outdated. 311 has no equivalent freshness signal.

4

Community Engagement

GCC cultivates an engaged community of reporters, verifiers, and volunteer cleaners through a gamified points and badge system. This community represents a free, self-sustaining monitoring workforce that generates data while improving their neighborhoods.

5

AI Analysis

On-device AI classification runs at the point of capture. Waste types are identified, severity is scored, and weight is estimated before the report is even submitted. This reduces manual triage and produces consistent, machine-comparable assessments across all reporters.

6

LiDAR Measurement

On devices equipped with LiDAR sensors (iPhone Pro, iPad Pro), GCC captures 3D depth scans of debris. This produces accurate volume and depth measurements for resource planning—data that is impossible to obtain from text-only 311 complaints.

7

Anti-Fraud

Device attestation, GPS coordinate locking, mandatory live camera capture (no gallery uploads), and session binding ensure every report is genuine. This level of anti-fraud protection means the city can trust the data without independent verification.

8

Cross-Reference Potential

GCC data can be spatially and temporally correlated with 311 sanitation complaints to identify underreported areas—neighborhoods with significant environmental issues that do not generate proportional 311 complaints. This fills blind spots in the city's existing data.

9

Cost Efficiency

KEY DIFFERENTIATOR

The city currently spends approximately $35–$50 per 311 complaint when accounting for call center operators, dispatch, and administrative overhead. GCC's community-driven model generates richer, more detailed data at zero cost to the city. Each GCC report contains 25+ structured fields, geotagged photos, and AI analysis — compared to the 5–8 fields in a typical 311 complaint. At scale, this represents millions of dollars in annual savings on environmental monitoring while producing superior data quality.

Proposed Arrangement

Three tiers of engagement, each building on the last. The City can begin with zero-cost data access and expand the relationship over time.

1

Data Sharing

ZERO COST

GCC publishes a public, SODA-compatible API. The City can pull data at any frequency using existing infrastructure. No API keys, no contracts, no engineering work required. This is available today.

✓ Public API access ✓ SODA-compatible format ✓ No cost to the City ✓ Available immediately
2

Official Integration

RECOMMENDED

The GCC dataset is listed on data.cityofchicago.org as a community-sourced companion to the existing 311 Sanitation Complaints dataset. The City provides official endorsement; GCC provides the data and maintains the API. Researchers and civic technologists gain a richer view of Chicago's environmental conditions.

✓ Listed on Data Portal ✓ City endorsement ✓ 311 cross-reference ✓ Researcher access
3

Partnership

FULL COLLABORATION

The City promotes GCC to residents as a tool for community environmental monitoring. GCC feeds real-time data to city dashboards. Both organizations pursue joint grant applications for environmental justice funding. The partnership creates a model for community-powered civic data that can scale to other domains.

✓ City promotion ✓ Dashboard integration ✓ Joint grant applications ✓ Environmental justice impact

90-Day Free Pilot Offer

We propose starting with a 90-day free pilot at the Professional tier (normally $5,000/month). During this period, the city evaluates data quality, coverage, API reliability, and integration feasibility with zero financial commitment. After the pilot, we negotiate a partnership tier based on demonstrated value. No contracts, no obligations — just proof through performance.
