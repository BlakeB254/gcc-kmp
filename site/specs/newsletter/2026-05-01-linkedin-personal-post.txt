Today, Global Community Cleaners (GCC) is dropping Issue #1 of our newsletter — and we're ramping up.

We're lining up the first 20 paid block cleanups in North Lawndale right now. That's 20% of our way to the goal we set for the pilot:

→ 100 blocks cleaned
→ 100 community members earning real money on their own street

The model is simple. $100 cleans one block. 100% of it goes to the resident doing the work. Same-day Stripe payout. One participant per block — gate to crest, both sides, including the alley. GPS-tagged before/after photos. Zero overhead pulled from the cleaning fee.

We're not running a charity drive — we're running a measurable civic-cleanliness program with real data. The GCC mobile app is live, and residents have already used it to map 110+ trash and dumping hotspots in our neighborhood, with photos and GPS coordinates. We hold the field evidence, we publish the patterns we see, and we put the money on the block.

📖 Issue #1 is here, with the full data, the academic studies behind the model, and our long-term infrastructure observations:
