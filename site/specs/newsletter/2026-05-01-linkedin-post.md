## Primary post (long-form, 2,000+ char LinkedIn limit)

Six years ago Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. moved his family into a third-floor walk-up on South Hamlin Avenue in North Lawndale to launch the action phase of the Chicago Freedom Movement.

Today, six blocks away on South Harding, **Global Community Cleaners (GCC) is preparing our first 20 block cleanups in North Lawndale.** That's 20% of our way to the goal we set for the pilot:

→ **100 blocks cleaned**
→ **100 community members earning real money on their own street**

Our model is simple. **$100 cleans one block. 100% of it goes to the resident doing the work.** Same-day pay. Elders and youth. One participant per block — gate to crest, both sides, including the alley. We document with before/after photos and we publish the data. The Branas trial in Philadelphia proved this kind of work cuts nearby gun assaults by 29% and depression rates by 41%. We're running the Chicago version.

We just dropped Issue #1 of our newsletter today, with the full data + the 110+ hotspots residents have already mapped in our app. Read it here: globalcommunitycleaners.org/blog/2026-05-01-issue-01

---

**Two things I'd ask my network for, plainly.**

**1) Donations on the cleanup side.** Every $100 puts a paid block on a North Lawndale street. If you've ever wished you could send dollars somewhere where every cent went to the work and not the overhead — this is that. globalcommunitycleaners.org/sponsor

**2) Local Chicago businesses — partner on our points system.** The GCC app lets anyone, anywhere, map and verify trash hotspots in their own neighborhood. Users earn points for it. We want to back those points with **real rewards from real local businesses**: sports/concert tickets, gift cards, restaurant credits, in-store discounts. If your brand wants documented community impact, photographable proof, and an audience that literally lives where you sell — email **hello@globalcommunitycleaners.org** with the subject line **"Partner on points"**.

**3) Help on the technical side.** I built GCC to serve a public good. We are not making a profit and we put everything we have into this. If anyone in my network can spare any of the following, it would massively accelerate what we can build:

  ▸ **An Android phone or tablet** — we currently don't have an Android device, which is blocking us from properly developing, testing, and shipping the Android version of the GCC app.
  ▸ **A laptop** — for the engineering team to do the Android dev work, plus general field operations.
  ▸ **Newer Mac systems** — for iOS builds and design work.
  ▸ **NVIDIA GPUs** — for the AI / image-classification side of our hotspot pipeline.
  ▸ **Cloud credits** — AWS, GCP, Vercel, Anthropic, OpenAI — anything that can extend our runway as we scale.

If you have any of the above sitting unused, or work at a company with a community-giving program that handles this kind of in-kind donation, please **DM me** or email **hello@globalcommunitycleaners.org**.

We're a registered 501(c)(3) (EIN 39-3553882). Every contribution is tax-deductible and every dollar / device / credit shows up on a real North Lawndale block.
