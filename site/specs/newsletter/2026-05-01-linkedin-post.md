## Primary post (long-form, founder voice)

Six decades ago Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. moved his family to a third-floor walk-up on South Hamlin Avenue in North Lawndale to launch the action phase of the Chicago Freedom Movement.

Today, six blocks away on South Harding, **Global Community Cleaners (GCC) is lining up the first 20 block cleanups in North Lawndale** — that's **20% of our way to the goal** we set for the pilot:

→ **100 blocks cleaned**
→ **100 community members earning real money on their own street**

Our model is simple. **$100 cleans one block. 100% of it goes to the resident doing the work.** Same-day pay. Elders and youth. One participant per block — gate to crest, both sides, including the alley. We document with before/after photos and we publish the data.

We just dropped Issue #1 of our newsletter today, with the full data + the 110+ hotspots residents have already mapped in our app:

📖 **Read it:** https://www.globalcommunitycleaners.org/blog/2026-05-01-issue-01

**Three asks I'm putting out to my network, plainly.**

**1) Donations on the cleanup side.** Every $100 puts a paid block on a North Lawndale street. If you've ever wished you could send dollars somewhere where every cent went to the work and not the overhead — this is that.
👉 globalcommunitycleaners.org/sponsor
We are also actively recruiting **recognized sponsors at the $2,000 tier** — name on our public sponsor wall, digital certificate of appreciation, 501(c)(3) tax receipt (EIN 39-3553882), monthly newsletter feature, and social-media recognition.

**2) Help on the personal/technical side so I can better serve my community and my network.** I built GCC to serve a public good. We are not making a profit and we put everything we have into this. If anyone in my network can spare any of the following — even gently used — it would massively accelerate what we can build:

  ▸ **An Android phone or tablet** — we currently don't have an Android device, which is blocking us from properly developing, testing, and shipping the Android version of the GCC app.
  ▸ **A laptop** — for the engineering team to do the Android dev work and field operations.
  ▸ **Newer Mac systems** — for iOS builds and design work.
  ▸ **NVIDIA GPUs** — for the AI / image-classification side of our hotspot pipeline.
  ▸ **Cloud credits** — AWS, GCP, Vercel, Anthropic, OpenAI — anything that extends our runway as we scale.

If you have any of the above sitting unused, or work at a company with a community-giving / in-kind donation program, please **DM me** or reply here.

**3) Spread it through your network — and if you're outside Chicago and want to launch this in your own community, reach out.**
GCC is a model that travels. The app is global, the funding mechanic is repeatable, and the impact data is measurable. **If you're in another neighborhood — in Chicago or anywhere else — and you're passionate about doing this for your block, your community, your city: I want to talk to you.** Sponsor leads from outside North Lawndale are equally welcome.

We built the GCC to serve a public good. Anything anyone can contribute — dollars, hardware, credits, a forwarded post, a referred sponsor — makes a real difference and is genuinely appreciated.
