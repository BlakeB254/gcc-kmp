## Part 3: Chicago Data Portal Partnership

### Who Manages the Chicago Data Portal?

- **Department**: Department of Technology and Innovation (DTI), formerly DoIT (Department of Innovation and Technology)
- **Key Role**: Chief Data Officer works with Chief Procurement Officer on data-related technology procurements
- **Platform**: Powered by Socrata (now Tyler Technologies); 99% of data arrives via automated ETL
- **Datasets**: 931+ datasets and counting
- **URL**: https://data.cityofchicago.org
- **City DTI Page**: https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/dti.html

### Has Chicago Paid for Third-Party Data?

- **No clear precedent** for purchasing third-party community-generated data
- Chicago's Open Data Executive Order (2012-2) mandates city data be published, but doesn't address incoming data
- **Procurement integration**: Contract provisions promote open data policies in technology-related procurements, suggesting openness to data partnerships
- **Smart Chicago Collaborative** (founding partners: City of Chicago, MacArthur Foundation, Chicago Community Trust) acts as fiscal agent for civic tech projects and provides infrastructure resources
- **Best approach**: Frame GCC data as filling a gap in existing city datasets, not as a product to purchase

### Chicago Department of Environment Budget

- **FY2026 Budget**: $47,954,637 (up from $45,918,684 in FY2025)
- **Effective operational budget**: ~$8.3M (84% of budget is Reserve Balance appropriation)
- **Federal grants**: $43.5M of $44.8M from EPA Superfund (Anadarko Streeterville Removal) -- not discretionary
- **Staffing expansion**: +65 budgeted positions in FY2026
- **Programs**: Energy Benchmarking transferred to DoE in 2025; Green Social Housing launched May 2025
- **URL**: https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/env.html
