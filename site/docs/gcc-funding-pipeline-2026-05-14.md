## Best Funding Positioning

GCC should not pitch itself as "trash pickup." The strongest funder frame is:

> A resident-paid, data-verified environmental justice and neighborhood safety platform that turns visible disorder into local income, public-health data, and measurable block-by-block improvement.

Three grant lanes should be run in parallel:

1. Environmental justice / public health: fund cleanup pilots, before/after documentation, environmental health reporting, and resident training.
2. Youth / workforce / civic power: fund paid youth and elder participation, neighborhood stewardship, third-space convenings, and policy/civic data loops.
3. Tech nonprofit infrastructure: fund maps, AI-assisted verification, donor acquisition, and data sharing with public/philanthropic partners.

## Reusable Proposal Skeleton

### Project Title

North Lawndale Block Environmental Health and Resident Workforce Pilot

### Short Summary

Global Community Cleaners NFP will pay North Lawndale residents to clean, document, and steward blocks in a concentrated pilot area around 1844 S. Harding Ave. Each cleanup is GPS/photo verified through GCC's app and produces before/after evidence, sponsor reporting, and neighborhood condition data. Funding will cover operations that block sponsorships do not cover: outreach, equipment, safety supplies, verification, software, reporting, insurance, data analysis, and community convenings.

### Need

North Lawndale faces visible environmental disorder, illegal dumping, vacant-lot trash, public-health stressors, and severe place-based inequities. GCC's model addresses the condition of the block and the economics of the block at the same time by paying residents directly for cleanup work while building a verifiable data layer that funders, institutions, and public agencies can use.

### Program Activities

- Recruit and onboard resident cleaners, with emphasis on youth, elders, and residents within the pilot radius.
- Equip participants with cleanup kits and safety guidance.
- Map hotspots and assign paid cleanup routes.
- Capture before/after photos and verification data in the GCC app.
- Pay residents promptly for completed cleanups.
- Publish monthly block impact reports for residents, sponsors, funders, and partners.
- Convene residents, institutional sponsors, funders, and civic stakeholders around the data and next-block priorities.

### Core Budget Buckets

- Resident outreach and onboarding
- Safety equipment and cleanup supplies
- Staff/admin/project management
- Insurance/compliance/accounting
- App hosting, data storage, mapping, and verification tooling
- Reporting/design/printing
- Community convenings
- Evaluation and data analysis

### Outcomes

- Number of paid resident cleanups completed
- Dollars paid directly to North Lawndale residents
- Number of blocks/hotspots documented before and after
- Number of youth/elder participants
- Pounds/bags of trash removed, if measured
- Sponsor/funder reports delivered
- Repeat condition score after 30/60/90 days
- Resident feedback and civic partner engagement
