North Lawndale · Sept 2026 · 501(c)(3) movement brief

North Lawndale is cleaning itself. Pay the people who live here.

Global Community Cleaners (GCC) is a 501(c)(3) at 1844 S. Harding Ave — six blocks from Dr. King’s 1966 walk-up at 1550 S. Hamlin. UIC’s Office of Community Collaboration funded the first 20 resident paychecks at $100/block ($2,000, 100% wages). Live site counters: 122 workers enrolled · 15 sponsors · 20/100 pilot blocks cleaned · $2,018 raised. Next: ~1,600 side-street blocks beyond Roosevelt, and an Android build (UIC is sponsoring a device) so the phones this neighborhood actually carries can run the same verified work.

How you can support us — two money tracks, plus in-kind. All gifts are 501(c)(3) tax-deductible (EIN 39-3553882).

Track A — Community labor (100% of this dollar hits a resident’s pocket). A $100 block sponsorship is not overhead. It is one paycheck, on their own street, typically 2–3 hours (4 hour cap), Stripe payout after photo/GPS/timestamp approval. That is the fastest legal income we can put on a North Lawndale block. It also starts a relationship — the same neighbors we pay become the people we can hire for events, deeper cleans, and youth/elder crews.

Recurring: weekly / biweekly / monthly block sponsorships keep the same resident pipeline paid. Volume: 10 / 50 / 100 blocks unlock 5% / 10% / 15% off add-ons only — worker pay never discounts.

Track B — Branding, services, events, media (this is how GCC itself stays open). $0 of the $100 block fee is kept. Add-ons and custom packages fund the app, insurance, Stripe fees, remaining supplies, the Android port, reconstruction compute, and — if we ever get there — a real salary so founder Shaquille Tate can quit the part-time jobs and prove a North Lawndale kid can make community work a career.

Build a package: Blocks → Branding (vests $15, shirts $12, hats $8, kit $30; brooms/rakes $10, bags $5, carts $25; yard signs $25, banners $50, stencil $75, chalk $100) → Services (deep +$50, pressure +$150, graffiti +$200, dumping +$300; snow $75, leaves $50; landscaping $100) → Events (pop-up $300 → large crew $1,500; block party $750, festival $2,000/day) → Media (photos $500–$7,500; video $1,000–$35,000). Enterprise from $25k (Community Champion) to $1M+ (Founding Partner).

Track C — In-kind (what cash still has to cover). Fields of Dreams Chicago — a North Lawndale youth org that meets at North Lawndale College Prep — gave our first in-kind gift on May 13, 2026 (~$500): 3 boxes gloves, 3 shovels, 4 dustpan-broom sets, 2 push brooms, 30-pack pickup sticks, dropped at the UIC Neighborhood Center. Still needed: rakes, contractor bags, high-vis vests, wheelbarrows, pressure washer, replacement gloves. Gifts in kind do not pay Stripe, insurance, phones, or people.

Why the data says this is the right North Lawndale bet

  • Long-term, not forever-loops: fenceline wind accumulation → windbreaks/screen + post-wind cadence. Pedestrian corridors with no cans for blocks → targeted bins. We clean and name the infrastructure that stops the next pile.
  • Branas RCT (Penn, PNAS / JAMA Network Open): greened/cleaned lots → 29.1% fewer gun assaults, 41.5% fewer depression reports, 68.7% drop for low-income residents. Municipal ROI $7.43 per public dollar on greening.
  • Sampson (Chicago): high collective efficacy → ~40% lower crime even when income/race/disorder match. A paid neighbor on their own block is efficacy with a receipt.
  • This neighborhood: 20.7-year life-expectancy gap (Loop 87.3 vs West Garfield Park 66.6). Black Chicagoans died 10.6 years earlier (CDPH 2023). North Lawndale ~3× city violent-crime rate (UIC Great Cities Institute). We are the block-level wage layer of that same fight.
  • App → human features: claim a funded block · two-lap guided route · AR/LiDAR scan · on-device litter ID · before/after photos · Stripe $100 · heatmap · bounties from $10 · points/badges. Under the hood: KMP + ARKit · R2 · Ktor on DGX · Neon/Next · Stripe. Android is the gap; UIC’s device is how we close it.
