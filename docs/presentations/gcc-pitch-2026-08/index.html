The Problem

Roosevelt Rd corridor, North Lawndale — photographed by our own scanners, July 2026.

Why it persists

The corridor stays dirty by design,
not by accident.

01

Dumping concentrates

Hotspots cluster along commercial corridors and vacant lots — the same blocks get hit again and again, faster than they recover.

02

City services can't keep pace

Municipal crews work ward-wide routes. Block-by-block problems fall through the gaps between scheduled sweeps.

03

No paid channel to help

Residents who want to clean their own street have no organized, compensated way to do it. Goodwill without infrastructure burns out.

The Solution

Pay residents to clean their own block —
and verify it.

1

Claim a funded block

Open the app, see sponsored blocks near you, claim one.

2

Clean it

Follow a guided two-lap route. Bags and gear provided.

3

Scan-verify

ARKit + on-device litter AI confirm the before/after.

4

Get paid

$100 per block pilot rate, straight to the resident.

The App

Built for the block, not the office.

Guided block routes

A two-lap scan flow walks the cleaner down both sides of the street, with a live coverage map showing what's done and what's left.

On-device litter ID

A CoreML classifier spots bottles, bags, and bulk dumping in real time, dropping AR anchors on every item it finds.

Scan-verified clean

The after-scan must match a clean block. Verified evidence is the payout trigger — no selfies, no honor system.

The Digital Twin

Every scan rebuilds
the block in 3D.

Each cleanup captures geolocated video, depth, and pose data. Reconstructions feed a living 3D twin of North Lawndale — a dataset no one else has.

  • City planning & analytics — dumping-hotspot trends, corridor condition over time.
  • Reconstruction-grade environment data — street-level 3D at repeat-visit fidelity.
  • A platform layer — jobs, gamification, and tax-credit partnerships on top.
919 scan artifacts and counting — R2 scan storage, July–Aug 2026.
The model scales

One mechanism, two winning sides.

For residents

  • Income from your own block — money for work that improves the street you live on.
  • Flexible & dignified — claim a block when it fits your day, no shifts, no boss.
  • Skills + references — a verified work history that travels with you.

For the city & sponsors

  • Measurably cleaner corridors — scan-verified outcomes, not self-reported hours.
  • Verified outcomes per dollar — every $100 maps to a proven clean block.
  • Sponsor a block for $100 — direct, local, and visible.
100-block pilot→ Gamified leagues→ Employer / tax-credit partnerships→ City-wide twin
