## How Points Work

Every action you take in the GCC app earns **Community Points (CP)**. Points reflect the real value of your contribution to the community. The harder or more selfless the action, the more points you earn.

### Core Point Values

| Action | Points | Why This Matters |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **Report a spot** | 50 CP | Your eyes keep the community informed |
| **Clean an unbountied spot** | 200 CP | Pure community service — no cash incentive, just heart |
| **Report AND clean the same spot** | 250 CP | The full lifecycle hero — you saw it, you fixed it |
| **Clean a bountied spot** | 75 CP | You earn the bounty payout too, so fewer points |
| **Complete a route** | 100 CP | Core job well done |
| **Post a public bounty** | 25 CP | Funding the community good |
| **Post a secret bounty** | 40 CP | Anonymous generosity deserves extra recognition |

### The GCC Philosophy

**Altruism pays more.** Cleaning a spot with no bounty earns **2.7x more points** than cleaning one with a cash reward. We believe the people who show up simply because it's the right thing to do deserve the most recognition.

## Bonus Multipliers

Stack these on top of your base points for even bigger rewards.

### Streak Bonuses

Show up consistently and your points multiply:

| Streak | Multiplier | Bonus |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| **3-day streak** | 1.2x | 20% more points on every action |
| **7-day streak** | 1.5x | 50% more points + **Week Warrior** badge |
| **30-day streak** | 2.0x | Double points + **Monthly Champion** badge |

*A streak = at least one cleanup action per day. Report a spot, clean a mess, complete a route — anything counts.*

### Situational Bonuses

Go above and beyond? We see you.

| Bonus | Multiplier | How to Earn |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Early Bird** | +50 CP flat | Clean a spot within 1 hour of it being reported |
| **Weekend Warrior** | +25% | Clean on Saturday or Sunday |
| **Weather Hero** | +30% | Clean during rain or snow |
| **Night Owl** | +20% | Clean between 6 PM and 6 AM |

*Bonuses stack. Clean during a snowstorm on a Sunday night with a 7-day streak? That's 1.5x base + 30% weather + 25% weekend + 20% night. Your neighbors will thank you — and your point total will show it.*

## Seasonal Challenges

Every month, GCC sets a **community-wide goal** that everyone works toward together.

**Example challenges:**
- "Clean 500 spots in North Lawndale this month"
- "Report 200 new spots across Chicago in February"
- "Complete 100 bounties before the snow melts"

### How Seasonal Challenges Work

1. A progress bar appears on your dashboard and the community info panel
2. Every participant's actions count toward the shared goal
3. When the community hits the target, **everyone** who contributed earns:
   - A limited-edition **Seasonal Champion** badge
   - Bonus CP (scaled to individual contribution)
   - Entry into that month's reward drawing

*Seasonal challenges create collective motivation. It's not just about your rank — it's about lifting the whole community together.*

## Anti-Gaming Protections

We keep the system fair for everyone.

- **Daily cap**: 2,000 CP per day maximum (prevents grinding exploits)
- **Self-report cooldown**: Can't report and immediately clean the same spot back-to-back more than 3 times per day
- **GPS verification**: Cleanups must happen at the reported location
- **Photo evidence**: Before and after photos required for cleanup verification
- **Community reporting**: Users can flag suspicious activity
